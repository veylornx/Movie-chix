About this directory

Open Reel only lists films that are in the public domain or released under a license that allows free sharing. Copyright rules differ between countries, so check a film's status where you live before reusing it. Every title links to its page on the Internet Archive, where the original rights information is shown.

Films and posters are hosted by the Internet Archive. Open Reel does not store any video files.
